## Why I built it?

There are three main reasons behind the creation of Intent <img width="16" height="16" alt="icon-128" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/36c91dad-59e0-4656-b24d-eb4efd2ad97a" />

#### First of all, to solve my specific personal needs.
I have been using a screen time control / digital health app on my mobile device for quite a while. 
<br> When I tried to find similar tools for my desktop browser(Safari), I wasn't able to find one that fit my needs perfectly.
<br> I also wanted an extension that supports Traditional Chinese
<br>(I couldn't find one that does. However, it may be that I just didn't search thoroughly enough.)


#### Secondly, this was an experiment for me.
I was wondering if LLMs & vibe coding could really produce something useful and of good quality.
<br>The answer is YES, but it's not as easy as it sounds. It took a lot of effort, constant testing, and debugging.


#### Lastly, my privacy concerns about existing similar extensions.
I had privacy concerns when other tools asked me to grant them access to websites.
<br>As a result, I built Intent with privacy in mind.


#### Hope you find Intent useful!
